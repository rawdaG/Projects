## 🎮 About the Game

You wake up at the start of a mysterious journey. At every turn, you're given a choice — go left or right, swim across or walk around, visit the house or the river. Each decision affects your **heart count (lives)**. Lose them all and it's over. Make it through and you win.

Simple premise. Surprisingly fun.

## 💬 Example Playthrough

```
Welcome to my first game!
What is your name? Rawda
What is your age? 22
You are old enough to play!
Do you want to play? yes

You are starting with 10 hearts ❤️
Let's play!

First choice...
Left or Right (left/right)? left
Nicely done, you follow the path and reach a lake...

Do you swim across or go around (across/around)? around
You went around and reached the other side of the lake.

You notice a house and a river.
Which do you go to (river/house)? river
You go to the river and find a boat. You sail away safely.

🎉 Congratulations, you survived! You have 10 hearts left.
```
